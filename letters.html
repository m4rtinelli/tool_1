Scatter positions

Ignores the original layout: drops each letter at a random spot and angle across the whole canvas, so big letters can overlap and bleed past the edges.

Align letters on baseline

Applies to "Import letters" (one SVG per letter) when Scatter is off. When unchecked, each letter gets a random vertical offset instead of sitting on one straight line.

Motion

Zero-G turbulence

Turns gravity off and pushes the letters through a swirling flow field so they drift and tumble around the frame continuously. The Gravity slider is ignored while this is on.
